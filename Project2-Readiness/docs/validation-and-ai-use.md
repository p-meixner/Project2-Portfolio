## Data and timing

`prices.csv` contains 61 monthly adjusted closes for SOXX, VTI, BND and SGOV, September 2021 through September 2026, downloaded October 22, 2026 from Yahoo Finance. The extraction used the last available trading-day adjusted close per calendar month; month-end labels need not be trading dates. The raw downloads and URLs are in [Lab15-data](../Lab15-data/sources.json). Monthly returns are percentage changes; the mean is multiplied by 12 and the sample monthly standard deviation by sqrt(12). Correlations use the same months. No missing prices are filled. This is a later download of adjusted history, not proof that every observation or adjustment was available at a historical decision date; no point-in-time backtest is claimed. The 36-return window requires 37 prices.

The separate DGS10 context is the last 10-year constant-maturity yield in the September 30, 2026 ALFRED vintage: September 29, 5.26% per year. Its source is Federal Reserve H.15 through [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10) and [ALFRED](https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/graph/alfredgraph.csv?id=DGS10&vintage_date=2026-09-30); a prior audit downloaded data October 29. [Lab 17](../Lab17.md) documents frequency, units, lag, revisions and missing values; the [saved audit](../Lab17-validation.json) records the comparison. A date-level vintage does not prove intraday availability. This signal is contextual and not used to select weights.
