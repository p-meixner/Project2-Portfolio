# Project 2 decision memo — draft for student review

OpenAI Codex assisted with code, calculations, and drafting; the student must review and own the analysis and decision. This is education, not investment advice.

**Committee action:** Reject adoption of the current historical-estimate optimization process for live capital. Consider a limited classroom pilot only after independently justified forward inputs, an agreed risk policy and stability rules, and implementation checks are approved. No actual allocation is authorized by this draft.

**Intended user and mandate.** A hypothetical committee assesses a long-term, Intel-industry-related fund portfolio using SOXX, VTI, BND and SGOV. The proposed classroom objective is at least 6.50% expected annual return after assumed costs; the portfolio is long-only, fully invested and reserves at least 5% in SGOV. Annualized volatility is minimized, with no established maximum volatility. Equal weight and inverse volatility are transparent analytical baselines. The app also shows a 60% VTI / 40% BND proxy for the Lab 13 benchmark; VTI is US rather than global equity and BND is not an exact investment-grade benchmark. This proposed mandate is not Intel's corporate investment policy and does not implement the complete Lab 13 Meridian mandate. The [reconciliation](mandate-reconciliation.md) maps each Meridian rule to the model and its gaps; the seven-year horizon, policy benchmark, acceptable loss measure and other requirements need proper implementation or approval.

**Evidence as of the saved dataset.** The 61 month-end adjusted price observations cover September 2021–September 2026 and were downloaded October 22, 2026. With zero assumed annual cost, the all-month solution is 10% SOXX / 0% VTI / 0% BND / 90% SGOV, expected return 6.81%, estimated volatility 3.84%, and 90% SGOV. Equal weight has 12.76% expected return, 13.69% volatility and passes the modeled checks; inverse volatility has 4.06% expected return, 1.32% volatility and fails the 6.50% return objective. The 60/40 proxy returns 7.71% with 11.38% estimated volatility and fails the example's reserve minimum. These are in-sample estimates, not forecasts or observed future returns. [Visible results](../project2-output.json).

**Range and stress.** Across the saved base, 36-month window, and one-point SGOV estimate stress, SOXX is 5–10%, VTI 0–10%, BND 0%, and SGOV 80–95%. This is an observed scenario range, not a recommended allocation band or a probability range. The 36-month solution is 5/0/0/95; reducing SGOV's return estimate one percentage point produces 10/10/0/80, a maximum 10-point weight move from the base. A 1% annual cost drag also gives 10/10/0/80; a 95% SGOV minimum leaves no feasible grid solution. The proposed stability tolerance is five points, so the 10-point stress breaches it. Cost is a simplified uniform return drag, not execution or turnover cost.

**Stop, monitor, and reverse.** Do not adopt if inputs are not defensible, any modeled rule fails, no feasible solution exists, or the proposed stability test exceeds five points. Monitor the forward-return assumptions, annual cost, reserve eligibility and liquidity, realized risk and allocation drift at least quarterly and when a material assumption changes. The DGS10 signal is a dated context observation, not a trading trigger. Reconsider the rejection only after a client approves the mandate and risk limits, forward assumptions are independently supported, costs and implementation constraints are tested, and the agreed stress rules pass. Even then, no return or loss is guaranteed.

**Committee ask:** decline live adoption, assign an owner to resolve the missing mandate and inputs, and review any classroom pilot separately. Validation, data limitations, AI disclosure, and the status of the locked record are in [validation and AI use](validation-and-ai-use.md).
