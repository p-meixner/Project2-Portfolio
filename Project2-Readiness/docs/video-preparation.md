# Video preparation — drafts, not transcripts

Record your own videos and correct their transcripts after recording. Each opens with an accurate AI-use statement and “education, not investment advice.” The scripts below are prompts for review, not evidence of a recording or a claim that the student personally performed an AI-off exercise.

## Video 1: Research evolution and AI check (maximum 3 minutes)

Open with your actual AI disclosure and ownership statement. Explain your initial thinking in your own words; Edition A and B are kept by you under the supplied project instructions, so do not invent an earlier view. Explain how the proposed classroom portfolio and decision evolved. Show one checked AI output: the AI-assisted Lab 20 objective change from 6.50% to 7.00%, committed before the run, changed the selected mix from 10/0/0/90 to 10/5/0/85. Explain that it was AI-assisted and is not the required AI-off record. Explain why you qualified the process after the SGOV stress moved weights by 10 points and how that affects your decision.

## Video 2: Technical walkthrough (maximum 5 minutes)

Open with your actual disclosure and ownership statement. Show the repository, dependency installation and app command. Explain how `prices.csv` feeds `estimate_from_csv`, how `search_portfolio` enumerates 5% weights and filters expected return and reserves before minimizing volatility, and how `app.py` displays the baselines and verdicts. Show a stress control and the reproduced output; explain one limitation, such as historical adjusted prices not proving point-in-time availability. Demonstrate a test you actually ran and know how to explain.

## Video 3: Committee presentation (maximum 5 minutes)

Open with the app running live and your accurate disclosure and ownership statement. Present the decision you actually approve. If retaining the memo's draft, say reject live adoption until forward assumptions and mandate risk limits are justified and stability conditions pass. Explain the 10/0/0/90 base allocation, 6.81% expected return, 3.84% volatility, and the 10-point SGOV stress shift; compare both baselines, describe monitoring and reversal, and close with the committee ask. Do not imply the observed scenario range is a recommended live allocation range or claim full mandate compliance.

Use separate public or unlisted YouTube links with captions enabled, review their access, and save a corrected transcript for each actual recording. Populate the manifest with working links, durations and evidence timestamps.
